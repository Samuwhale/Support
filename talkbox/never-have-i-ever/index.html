One person reads a statement beginning with “Never have I ever”. Anyone who has done it raises a hand. There is no need to keep score; the conversation is the point.

  1. Read one statement to the group.
  2. Everyone who has done it raises a hand.
  3. Leave room for a story, then pass the phone and try another.

24 Never Have I Ever prompts

Everyone answers the same statement. Let people decide how much of the story they want to tell.

  3. Never have I ever accidentally liked a very old photo.

Before you play

How many people do we need?

You can play with two people, though a group gives you more stories to discover. The app supports 2–8 players.

Do we need to drink or keep score?

Neither is required. This sampler is a way to start stories. Choose rules everyone agrees with.

Are these the Hot questions?

No. These examples come from Talkbox’s Light deck. The app also has an optional Hot selection for adults who choose it.
