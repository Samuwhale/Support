Let a question
be a beginning.

You do not need to work through a list in order. Choose a question that fits the people you are with. When an answer turns into a story, stay with it instead of rushing to the next prompt.

  1. Start with a playful question when the group is still warming up.
  2. Ask “why that one?” or “what changed?” when someone gives a short answer.
  3. Move to a more personal question only when everyone wants to. Passing is always fine.

24 questions for your next conversation

These examples come from Talkbox’s Fun, Deep and Nostalgia categories. Player-name openings are omitted so anyone can read them aloud.

  1. What business would you start if being useful was optional?
  2. If your life was a Netflix show, what would it be called?
  3. What's your first move in a zombie apocalypse?
  4. What video game would you absolutely dominate?
  5. What movie sound effect should be replaced with your voice?
  6. What's your worst app idea that someone would download?
  7. What animal would cause chaos if it was horse-sized?
  8. What's the most dramatic moment in your daily routine?
  9. Has there been a belief in your life that recently changed?
  10. What conversation changed a decision you were about to make?
  11. When did sticking to a principle cost you something?
  12. What habit would you be glad to see a younger person pick up from you?
  13. What advice made more sense once you had lived on your own?
  14. What have you recently made time for that used to come last?
  15. What question would you ask if you were guaranteed a true answer?
  16. When did a stranger make a difficult day easier?
  17. What object would immediately tell someone which years you grew up in?
  18. What toy completely defined your childhood?
  19. What song instantly transports you to middle school?
  20. What childhood myth did you believe way too long?
  21. What family tradition do you miss most?
  22. What smell instantly brings back childhood memories?
  23. What was on your childhood bedroom walls?
  24. What's your best childhood birthday memory?

Before you play

Can we use these on a date?

Yes. These questions do not assume you are already a couple or have shared history. Start light and choose deeper questions together.

Can a whole group play?

Yes. Read the same question to everyone, or take turns choosing someone to answer. The app supports 2–8 players.

Are answers stored?

This page does not ask you to enter answers or player names. Your conversation stays in the room. The app also does not record your answers.
